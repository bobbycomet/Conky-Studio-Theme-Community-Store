Themes for Conky Studio, from the community and beyond.

A catalog, not an installer. Every card here links out to wherever the theme actually lives. GitHub, Pling, KDE Store, openDesktop, wherever the author put it.

Theme Vault never downloads or runs anything on your machine. Click through, read the README, then grab it from the source the same way you'd grab any theme you didn't build yourself.
